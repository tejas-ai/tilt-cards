# Tilt Cards Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Tilt Cards is an Android live wallpaper app maintained by Tejas Handigol. This policy describes the current Android app, including the Preview build with Auto Rotate.

## Photos and settings

Tilt Cards reads the photos you select using Android's photo picker or file picker. It copies the selected photos into the app's private storage and uses them for previews and wallpapers. The app stores your wallpaper selections, mode, power setting, and rotation schedule on your device.

## Motion sensors

When motion is enabled, Tilt Cards uses available device motion sensors to detect tilt and switch or animate wallpapers. It processes sensor readings on your device. The app can vibrate briefly when a tilt changes the selected wallpaper.

## Collection and sharing

The current app has no sign-in, analytics, or advertising integration. It does not send your photos, wallpaper settings, or motion readings to the developer or to any server. If you open a creator profile link, Android opens the external destination, which has its own privacy policy.

## Pinterest integration

Pinterest integration is proposed and is not active in the current app. The intended initial test would use read-only authorization to display images from a board selected by the signed-in account holder as scheduled Android wallpapers. We will update this policy before enabling the integration to explain what data it accesses, how it is used and retained, and how to disconnect it. The integration will use the access and handling permitted by Pinterest.

## Retention and deletion

Tilt Cards keeps its local copies while they are in your collection. Choose **Use demo designs** to clear the current mode's saved collection, or clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove its stored photos and settings. Your original gallery photos are separate from the app's copies.
